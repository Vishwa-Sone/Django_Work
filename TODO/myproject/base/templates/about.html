About Our TODO App

Welcome to TODO App, a simple and organized way to manage your daily tasks. This application helps you keep track of your work, remember important activities, and stay focused on what needs to be done.

You can add new tasks, update existing tasks, mark tasks as completed, and move tasks to the trash when they are no longer needed. Everything is designed to make managing your everyday activities easier.

Whether you are managing college assignments, personal goals, or daily responsibilities, TODO App helps you organize your work in one place.

**Our goal:** To make task management simple, clear, and efficient, so you can spend less time organizing your work and more time getting things done.
